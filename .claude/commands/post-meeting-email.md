# Purpose
Draft a client-facing follow-up email after a stakeholder meeting.

# Prompt
Write a concise, professional email that summarizes outcomes and next steps.

Steps:
1. Capture meeting purpose and key outcomes.
2. Confirm decisions made and unresolved questions.
3. List action items with owners and due dates.
4. Note risks/escalations in neutral language.
5. End with a clear call to action.

Tone requirements:
- Client-safe, direct, and respectful
- Plain language for technically literate non-engineers
- No internal-only shorthand

# Expected inputs
- Meeting notes or transcript
- Recipient audience (sponsor, steering committee, workstream leads)
- Desired send date

# Example output
Subject: Follow-up: Data Warehouse Steering Committee (8 May 2026)

Hi team,

Thank you for today’s steering committee discussion. We confirmed completion of finance and sales KPI mapping and agreed to run CRM integration testing in parallel to protect timeline confidence.

Key actions:
- Client IT to confirm ERP API credential provisioning by 12 May (Owner: Client IT Lead)
- PMO to publish revised UAT schedule by 13 May (Owner: Priya)
- Client Ops to provide supplier dedupe rule by 12 May (Owner: Ops Manager)

Open decision required: please confirm whether weekend cutover support will be handled via a formal change request by 15 May.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
