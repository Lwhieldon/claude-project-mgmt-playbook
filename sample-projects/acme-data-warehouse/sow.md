# ACME Data Warehouse Modernization - SOW Excerpt (Fictional)

## Objective
Design and implement a cloud data warehouse to consolidate finance, sales, and supply chain reporting for ACME Manufacturing.

## In scope
- Discovery workshops across 3 business domains
- Data model design for 22 priority KPIs
- ETL/ELT pipelines for 11 source systems
- UAT support and go-live hypercare (2 weeks)

## Out of scope
- Source system remediation
- Long-term managed services after hypercare

## Key milestones
| Milestone | Target date | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery sign-off | 2026-04-30 | Client Program Lead |
| Data model baseline complete | 2026-05-20 | Consulting Data Architect |
| UAT start | 2026-06-03 | Joint PMO |
| Go-live | 2026-07-17 | Steering Committee |

## Assumptions
- Client provides environment access within 5 business days of request.
- Business testers are available 30% during UAT.
- API credentials for ERP and CRM are provisioned before integration testing.

## Risks noted at contract signature
- Legacy source quality is inconsistent for supplier master data.
- Cutover may require weekend support not currently budgeted.
