## 1. Project Overview

Globex Industries ("Client") is replacing its existing SAP ECC 6.0 system (in production since 2009) with SAP S/4HANA 2023 using a "clean core" approach. The implementation will cover the Finance (FI/CO), Manufacturing (PP/QM), Procurement (MM/SRM), and Sales & Distribution (SD) modules. The project will run in three phases over 18 months, concluding with a phased go-live beginning April 2027.

This engagement is scoped as Time & Materials with a monthly cap. The Firm will provide a dedicated project team of 8–12 consultants, including functional leads, technical architects, change management support, and a Project Manager.

---

## 2. Scope of Work

### 2.1 Phase 1: Prepare and Blueprint (Months 1-5, Jan-May 2026)

- Current-state process documentation across all four modules
- Future-state business process design workshops (standard SAP processes preferred)
- System landscape design and technical architecture
- Data migration strategy and legacy data assessment
- Integration architecture for Globex's external EDI partners (4 partners)
- Fit-gap analysis with documented design decisions
- Blueprint Document, approved by Client sponsors

### 2.2 Phase 2: Realize (Months 6-14, Jun 2026 - Feb 2027)

- System configuration per approved Blueprint
- Custom development (RICEFW objects; limited to items identified in fit-gap; target ≤ 15 custom objects)
- Data migration development (data extraction, transformation, and load scripts)
- Integration development and testing for all 4 EDI partners
- Unit testing and integration testing
- User Acceptance Testing (UAT) facilitation, covering two full UAT cycles
- Cutover planning

### 2.3 Phase 3: Deploy and Stabilize (Months 15-18, Mar-Jun 2027)

- Mock cutover (2 rehearsals minimum)
- Production go-live, phased by business unit (Manufacturing first, then Distribution, then Finance close)
- Hypercare support: 60 days post each go-live phase, included in contract
- Knowledge transfer to Client IT team
- Final documentation package

### 2.4 Out of Scope

- Any SAP modules not listed above (e.g., HR/SuccessFactors, Ariba, CRM)
- Custom development beyond the RICEFW objects identified in the Phase 1 fit-gap analysis
- Infrastructure procurement (Client to procure SAP RISE or BTP environment directly)
- Business process changes not related to the SAP implementation
- Training content development (see Section 7 for training approach)

## 4. Assumptions

1. The Client will provide dedicated SME participation from Finance, Manufacturing, Procurement, and Sales, estimated at 20-30% of SME time throughout Phases 1 and 2.
2. The Client will procure SAP RISE (cloud ERP) licenses and ensure environments (Development, QA, Production) are available by March 1, 2026.
3. The number of RICEFW custom objects will not exceed 15, as established in the fit-gap analysis. Objects exceeding this count are subject to a change request.
4. EDI partner integrations are limited to 4 partners currently in scope. Additional partners require a change request.
5. The Client's existing data is of acceptable quality for migration. A data profiling assessment in Phase 1 will surface any material quality issues.
6. Globex's internal IT team will manage ongoing system administration post-hypercare.
7. All business process design decisions will be made by Client sponsors in a timely manner; delays exceeding 10 business days will be reflected in timeline adjustments.
