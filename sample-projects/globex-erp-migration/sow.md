# Globex ERP Migration - SOW Excerpt (Fictional)

## Objective
Migrate Globex Services from legacy ERP to a cloud ERP platform across finance and procurement.

## In scope
- Process design for procure-to-pay and record-to-report
- Data migration for 6 years of financial history
- Integrations with payroll, CRM, and banking interfaces
- Training for 140 end users

## Out of scope
- HR module redesign
- Post-go-live enhancements beyond 30-day stabilization

| Milestone | Date | Owner |

Fixed fee of $1.2M with CR-based changes outside defined scope.
